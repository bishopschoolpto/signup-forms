Organizers: set up a sign-up

Each event is a Google Sheet in the PTO's Events folder. Fill in the sheet, then get a link to share.

  2. Copy the template or an old event

    Open Event Template, or a past event like yours, and choose File → Make a copy. Name it after your event.

    • Keep the copy directly in the Events folder, not inside Past events or another folder. Copies of past events land in Past events, so move yours to Events.
    • Copied an old event? On the Signups tab, delete every row except the header row. Its old short link and QR code are replaced when you make a new one in step 6.

  4. Add your slots on the Slots tab

    One row per job or shift. capacity is how many volunteers it needs. Type dates and times like 10/23/2026 9:00 AM.

  5. Ask volunteers questions (optional)

    Show

    Need a T-shirt size, allergies, or a background-check confirmation? Add one row per question on the Questions tab. They appear on the sign-up form, and each answer gets its own column in the Signups tab.

    Questions tab
    ABCD
    1questiontypeoptionsrequired
    2T-shirt sizechoiceS, M, L, XLyes
    3Food allergies?paragraphno
    4I've completed the district background checkcheckboxyes
    • type: text (short answer, the default), paragraph (longer answer), choice (pick one of the options, separated by commas) or checkbox (a box to tick).
    • required: yes means volunteers can't sign up without answering (for a checkbox: without ticking it).
    • Up to 10 questions. Avoid renaming a question after people have answered it: the new name starts a new column.
    • No Questions tab? Add a tab named Questions with those four column names in row 1.

  6. Personalize the emails (optional)

    Show

    The volunteer's email. Every volunteer gets an email with their slot, time and place. Use the confirmationSubject and confirmationMessage rows to change its subject and add your own message, such as parking, what to bring or what to wear.

    Your email. If you set organizerEmail, you get an email for each sign-up with the volunteer's details. Use organizerSubject to give it a subject that's easy to filter, like [Book Fair] {name} – {slot}, and organizerMessage for a reminder to yourself, like "Add them to the volunteer group chat". Waitlist sign-ups keep "Waitlist:" in front.

    • These words are replaced with each volunteer's details, in both emails: {firstName} {name} {email} {phone} {event} {slot} {when} {location}. A blank phone number becomes nothing.
    • For a new line in the message, press Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) inside the cell. Web links become clickable.
    • Don't see these rows? Add them at the bottom of the Event tab: the name in column A, your text in column B.
    • To try it out, sign up for your own event, then delete your row from the Signups tab.

  2. Watch sign-ups on the Signups tab

    Each sign-up adds a row. The app writes this tab, so please only change the status column. The sign-up page only shows first names and last initials.

    Signups tab
    ABCDEFGH
    1signupIdslotIdslotLabelnameemailphonenotestatus
    2988640f1-…setupSetupJane Doejane@example.com555-0100Happy to help!confirmed
    34c1d27a9-…setupSetupSam Roesam@example.comwaitlisted
    • When every slot is full, volunteers can join a slot's waitlist. They show up here with status waitlisted and don't take a spot.
    • Volunteers can cancel with the link in their confirmation email. Their status becomes cancelled, and if the slot has a waitlist, the next person in line automatically gets the spot (status confirmed) and an email. You get an email either way.
    • Changing a status yourself doesn't move anyone up. If you cancel someone by hand, give their spot to the next waitlisted person by changing their status to confirmed, and let them know.

  3. Close sign-ups when you're done

    Move your spreadsheet into the Past events folder (inside Events). New sign-ups stop right away. The sign-up page still shows who signed up, with a note that sign-ups are closed.
